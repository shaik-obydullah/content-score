      How the score works

      Content depth · 20%

      Word count and approximate reading time. Thin pages score lower.

      Readability · 20%

      Flesch Reading Ease, targeting the 60–70 range that suits web readers.

      Structure · 15%

      One H1, sensible heading density, a clean hierarchy and descriptive headings.

      Topic focus · 15%

      Whether the main term appears in the title, H1, intro and body at a healthy density.

      SEO basics · 15%

      Title, meta description, canonical, language, viewport and HTTPS.

      Media & links · 10%

      Image alt coverage plus internal and external linking.

      Technical · 5%

      HTTPS, structured data, Open Graph tags and favicon.

      This is a heuristic score, not an official ranking factor. Treat it as a checklist — the recommendations matter more than the exact number.
